# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

### ✅ Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- **Being respectful**: Treating all community members with dignity and respect
- **Being inclusive**: Welcoming newcomers and helping them feel part of the community
- **Being constructive**: Providing helpful feedback and suggestions
- **Being collaborative**: Working together towards common goals
- **Being patient**: Understanding that everyone has different skill levels and backgrounds
- **Being empathetic**: Showing understanding and compassion for others' perspectives
- **Being professional**: Maintaining appropriate conduct in all interactions
- **Giving credit**: Acknowledging others' contributions and ideas
- **Being open-minded**: Considering different viewpoints and approaches
- **Taking responsibility**: Owning mistakes and learning from them

### ❌ Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- **Harassment**: Any form of harassment, intimidation, or unwelcome attention
- **Discrimination**: Prejudicial treatment based on personal characteristics
- **Trolling**: Deliberately inflammatory or disruptive behavior
- **Personal attacks**: Insulting or derogatory comments about individuals
- **Doxxing**: Publishing private information without consent
- **Spam**: Excessive self-promotion or off-topic content
- **Plagiarism**: Using others' work without proper attribution
- **Threats**: Any form of threatening behavior or language
- **Inappropriate content**: Sexual, violent, or otherwise inappropriate material
- **Disruption**: Deliberately disrupting discussions or community activities

## Our Responsibilities

### Community Leaders

Community leaders are responsible for:

- **Clarifying standards**: Explaining and enforcing community standards
- **Fair enforcement**: Applying consequences fairly and consistently
- **Prompt response**: Addressing violations in a timely manner
- **Transparency**: Being open about enforcement decisions when appropriate
- **Education**: Helping community members understand expectations
- **Improvement**: Continuously working to improve the community environment

### All Community Members

All community members are responsible for:

- **Following guidelines**: Adhering to this Code of Conduct
- **Reporting violations**: Alerting leaders to inappropriate behavior
- **Supporting others**: Helping create a welcoming environment
- **Self-reflection**: Considering the impact of their actions on others
- **Continuous learning**: Being open to feedback and growth

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all community spaces, including:

### Online Spaces

- GitHub repositories and discussions
- Issue trackers and pull requests
- Community forums and chat platforms
- Social media interactions related to the project
- Email communications

### Offline Spaces

- Conferences and meetups

- Speaking at conferences or events
- Posting on social media as a project representative
- Acting as an appointed representative at online or offline events

## Enforcement

### Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by:

1. **Direct contact**: Email the maintainers at [conduct@ideasui.com](mailto:conduct@ideasui.com)
2. **GitHub**: Use the "Report abuse" feature on GitHub
3. **Private message**: Contact any maintainer directly

### What to Include in Reports

When reporting violations, please include:

- Your contact information (if comfortable sharing)
- Names of individuals involved
- Description of the incident
- Date and time of occurrence
- Any relevant screenshots or documentation
- Any witnesses to the incident

### Response Process

1. **Acknowledgment**: Reports will be acknowledged within 24 hours
2. **Investigation**: Thorough and fair investigation of the incident
3. **Decision**: Determination of appropriate consequences
4. **Communication**: Response to the reporter with outcome
5. **Follow-up**: Monitoring to ensure no retaliation occurs

### Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will follow these guidelines when determining consequences:

#### 1. Correction

**Community Impact**: Use of inappropriate language or other behavior deemed unprofessional.

**Consequence**: A private, written warning providing clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

#### 2. Warning

**Community Impact**: A violation through a single incident or series of actions.

**Consequence**: A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with the people involved for a specified period. This includes avoiding interactions in community spaces as well as external channels like social media.

#### 3. Temporary Ban

**Community Impact**: A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence**: A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period. No public or private interaction with the people involved is allowed during this period.

#### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community Impact**: Demonstrating a pattern of violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment, or aggression toward individuals or classes of individuals.

**Consequence**: A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the community.

## Appeals Process

If you believe you have been unfairly penalized:

1. **Submit appeal**: Email [appeals@ideasui.com](mailto:appeals@ideasui.com) within 30 days
2. **Include information**: Provide detailed explanation of why you believe the decision was unfair
3. **Review process**: Appeals will be reviewed by different maintainers than those involved in the original decision
4. **Final decision**: Appeals decisions are final

## Privacy and Confidentiality

- **Reporter privacy**: We will protect the privacy of those who report violations
- **Confidential handling**: All reports will be handled confidentially
- **Limited disclosure**: Information will only be shared with those necessary for investigation
- **No retaliation**: We have zero tolerance for retaliation against reporters

## Community Values

### Diversity and Inclusion

We celebrate diversity and strive to create an inclusive environment where everyone can contribute their unique perspectives and skills.

### Learning and Growth

We encourage continuous learning and view mistakes as opportunities for growth rather than reasons for punishment.

### Quality and Excellence

We maintain high standards for code quality while being supportive of contributors at all skill levels.

### Collaboration over Competition

We prioritize collaborative problem-solving over individual recognition.

### Transparency and Openness

We operate with transparency in our processes and decision-making while respecting privacy when appropriate.
